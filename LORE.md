# The Last Monster

Grak wakes in a ruined village. No one answers when he calls. Across the river,
he finds the same destruction, so he heads for the capital.

The city is mostly intact, but its streets are empty. Beneath the Rotunda he
finds Malgrath, a dragon beside his hoard. The heroes' guild burned the villages
and tried to take the city. Malgrath drove them back, but barely made it down
the stairs afterward. He cannot fight another attack.

Grak knows how to build. He needs timber, iron, weapons, and gold to pay for
them. Malgrath lets him take the coins beside the steps. The rest stay where
they are.

## The defense

Grak fortifies the approaches with the kin who arrive to help. The guild sends
twenty expeditions against each defense. Fighters who get through strike
Malgrath; the heart count is his remaining health.

The Rotunda leads to the Rift, the Long Halls, and the Sunken Garden. Holding
all four on the same renown opens the Heart. Beyond that lie the Unmapped
Depths, whose passages change with each new route.

Grak's journal records the defenders, the guild fighters, the places he visits,
and what happens after the fighting. Those pages are discovered through play.
